🎓 Student Discipline

  • Students should be respectful, polite and responsible.
  • Students must follow instructions from teachers and school authorities.
  • Bullying, harassment and disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

👔 Uniform & Appearance

  • Students should attend school in the prescribed uniform.
  • Students should maintain a neat and clean appearance.

📚 Attendance & Punctuality

  • Students should arrive on time and attend classes regularly.
  • Parents/guardians should inform the school about unavoidable absence.
